# The Simply Sexy Cigars Editorial Review Rubric

*Derived from the Editorial Style Guide v1.0. The pre-publication review for every Journal article, Edition feature, homepage editorial, and brand story.*

**Version 1.0 — Frozen, 26 July 2026.** The permanent editorial review standard. Do not revise unless a Version 2 is explicitly requested. Image and technical requirements live in the companion `EDITORIAL-PRODUCTION-CHECKLIST.md`; the governing document is `EDITORIAL-STYLE-GUIDE.md`.

An article advances only once it earns the tier **Publication Ready** (or **Flagship**).

---

## How to use it (5–10 minutes)

1. **Read the piece once, aloud** if possible — rhythm and false notes only surface in the ear.
2. **Check the Gates.** Any single failure stops the review: the piece is **Hold**, no further assessment needed.
3. **Assess the ten Dimensions** — mark each *Exemplary · Solid · Weak · Absent.* No numbers; this is editorial judgment, not arithmetic.
4. **Apply The Editor's Test and the final question**, assign the **tier**, and record the **Top 3 fixes.**

---

## Part A — The Gates *(binary; all must PASS)*

Fast, non-negotiable checks from the guide's Kill List and house rules. **Any FAIL → Hold**, whatever the writing's quality.

- [ ] **No selling inside the story.** No prices, packages, or call-to-action within the piece.
- [ ] **Brand held to the edges.** The name never appears mid-story — entrance voice, a single exit line, or the signature only.
- [ ] **No Kill-List elements.** No superlatives/marketing adjectives, clichés, emoji, exclamation points, testimonials-as-features, share/tag/archive furniture, countdowns, volume numbers, or author bios.
- [ ] **No list that narrows the audience.** Occasions are evoked through shared feeling, not enumerated.
- [ ] **Timeless.** No dates, trends, current references, or hemisphere-specific assumptions in the prose.
- [ ] **Terminology correct.** *Edition* (not Issue/Collection); named season, no volume number; *The Journal*, *The Moment*; tagline only in the signature.
- [ ] **Structure intact.** Masthead · drop-cap opening · chaptered body · **exactly one** pull quote · **The Moment** block · a short lingering close. Runs 5–7 minutes.

---

## Part B — The Ten Dimensions *(mark each: Exemplary · Solid · Weak · Absent)*

| # | Dimension | Exemplary looks like | Guide source |
|---|-----------|----------------------|--------------|
| 1 | **Sense of place** | The first paragraph transports the reader into the season before explaining anything. | Principle 2 |
| 2 | **Sensory fullness** | All five senses present across the piece — smell, sound, taste, touch, light — each deepening atmosphere, none decorative. | Principle 3 |
| 3 | **Observed, not invented** | Reads as *remembered*; specifics feel lived; shows rather than tells. | Principle 4 |
| 4 | **Universality** | The reader pictures *their own* celebration; no one is left outside the story. | Principle 5 · Two-Lens |
| 5 | **Restraint & inevitability** | Every sentence earns its place; nothing padded; shorter than it wants to be. | Principle 1 |
| 6 | **Rhythm** | Reads aloud with varied sentence length and true stillness; no rule-of-three monotony. | Principle 6 |
| 7 | **Brand almost disappears** | Feeling first, recognition second; the house is felt, not announced. | Principle 7 · Two-Lens |
| 8 | **Thematic fidelity** | Quietly serves the seven themes (slowing down, gathering, hospitality, memory, ritual, lingering, celebration); never *about* cigars. | What It's Really About |
| 9 | **Voice & diction** | The unhurried host; present tense, second person; no marketing adjectives or clichés. | The Voice |
| 10 | **The ending lingers** | Closes on feeling, not thesis; leaves the reader sitting still because it feels *true*. | Principle 10 |

## The Publication Tiers

No scores. The tier is an editorial judgment, assigned from the state of the Gates and Dimensions.

| Tier | Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Flagship** | Gates pass · every Dimension *Solid* or better, with **Sense of place, Sensory fullness, and The ending** all *Exemplary* · The Editor's Test and the final question both an emphatic *yes* | Defines the voice; a reference text (e.g. *Autumn Is Cigar Season*). Fit to lead the Edition. |
| **Publication Ready** | Gates pass · **no Dimension marked *Weak* or *Absent*** · The Editor's Test passes · the final question is *yes* | Publishable as-is. |
| **Revise** | Gates pass · one or more Dimensions *Weak* (none *Absent*) | Close. Return with the Top 3 fixes, then re-review. |
| **Hold** | Any Gate fails · any Dimension *Absent* · or The Editor's Test / final question fails | Rework before re-review. |

An article advances to the next only at **Publication Ready** or **Flagship.**

---

## The Editor's Test

*(Overrides any tier assignment — the piece can be technically clean and still not belong to us.)*

> Would every sentence survive, unchanged, in **Robb Report, Four Seasons Magazine, Cigar Aficionado, or Departures**?

If the answer is no, the piece is not ready, whatever the Dimensions say.

---

## The Last Question

Ask it after everything else, alone, honestly. It is the whole standard in six words:

> ### *Would I reread this next autumn?*

If the answer is not *yes* — not "it's fine," not "probably," but *yes* — the piece is not yet Publication Ready.
