A Note for the Season

Some seasons send us out into the world. Autumn brings us back in.

It arrives like an old friend — the low gold light, the first fire of the year, a table laid for the people you most want to see. There is a quiet generosity to the season, and it asks for very little: your company, and a little of your time.

So come in. Pour something you love. Take the seat nearest the warmth.

You have arrived exactly where you hoped to be.

— The Editors · Simply Sexy Cigars
