Golf Tournaments · The Experience

A Different Kind of Stop on the Course.

Golfers come for the tournament. Somewhere on the course, they come across something they didn't expect: an attended cigar experience, and a place to pause and gather in the middle of the round.

Northern California On the Course · At the Gathering
Begin the Conversation

A tournament has a rhythm all its own: the shotgun start, the long middle holes, the pull toward the clubhouse at the end. What players remember, and mention to the friend they invite next year, is rarely a score. It is the moment the day surprised them, an unhurried pause when the round slowed down and the group actually talked.

An attended cigar experience gives a tournament exactly that. It is hospitality, offered where the day is already happening, and it can become one of the details players remember. This is the page for the people who shape those days: tournament organizers, charity committees, country clubs, corporate hosts, event planners, and the sponsors arranging a guest experience.

Two Ways to Bring It

On the Course, or at the Gathering.

On the course.

The mobile cart, a black Club Car with a glass-front humidor cabinet, can meet golfers at a selected hole during the tournament. An ambassador presents the cigars and helps each golfer choose, cut, and light, an unexpected pause in the middle of the round.

At the gathering.

An attended cigar table can be incorporated into the tournament gathering, wherever your event brings people together. It gives the experience a home once the round is over, a place where players linger and the day winds down together.

Many events choose one. Some use both: the cart out on the course, the tended table once everyone comes in.

The Stop They Didn't See Coming.

There is a particular pleasure in rounding a corner mid-round and finding the cart waiting: the cabinet open and warmly lit, an ambassador ready, a cigar chosen and lit for the walk to the next tee. It breaks the rhythm of the day in the best way, a few unhurried minutes that a foursome carries with them through the back nine.

Set at a selected hole during the tournament, it can become one of the moments players remember from the day. It is the kind of detail a tournament is remembered by, and a thoughtful way for a host to care for their guests.

How It Works

From First Note to Final Light.

I

The conversation

We begin by learning your tournament: the format, the course, the number of players, and the feeling you want the day to leave. Your committee or club is welcome at every step.

II

The selection

We help you choose a selection suited to the occasion, your guests, and the budget you have in mind, with one thoughtfully selected cigar for every guest.

III

The day

We arrive, place the cart on the course or set the tended table, and an ambassador stays with the experience throughout, helping each golfer choose, cut, and light.

IV

The story

Your players gather, linger, and carry the day with them. We work with you to decide where the experience fits naturally into the tournament day.

Welcoming to Everyone.

A cigar experience at a tournament is not only for the players who already know cigars. Many of the people who gather have never held one, and that is part of the appeal. The ambassador is there to make it easy: to help each guest choose, to cut and light by hand, and to make a first cigar feel comfortable rather than intimidating.

Most golfers come for the pause and the conversation as much as the cigar itself. It is an open, welcoming stop in the day, and everyone in the field is invited into it.

When the Tournament Is a Fundraiser.

Many of the best golf days are built for a cause. The experience is one of the most natural fits for a charity scramble or benefit tournament: it gives players an unexpected moment to gather around, and it belongs to the cause the moment it arrives.

If the fundraiser itself is the focus rather than the round, our Charitable Events experience speaks to galas, benefit dinners, and donor gatherings more broadly.

Yes. The cart can meet golfers at a selected hole during the tournament, so every group finds it as they come through, a natural pause in the day. Where it goes is something we plan with you.

Yes. An attended cigar table can be incorporated into the tournament gathering, so the experience continues once the round is over. Some events use the cart on the course and an attended table at the gathering.
Do golfers need cigar experience?
Not at all. Many golfers who gather have never had a cigar. Our ambassadors help each guest choose, cut, and light, and make first-timers feel comfortable from the start. Most people come for the pause and the conversation as much as the cigar.
How are cigars selected?
Every event begins with a planning conversation. We work with you to choose a selection suited to the occasion, your guests, and the budget you have in mind, with one thoughtfully selected cigar for every guest.
Can this work for charity golf tournaments?
Yes, and it is one of the most natural fits. The experience gives players an unexpected moment to gather around, and it belongs to the cause the moment it arrives. For fundraising events more broadly, see our Charitable Events experience.
Where does Simply Sexy Cigars serve golf events?
We serve Northern California, from San Francisco and Silicon Valley to Napa, Sonoma, Sacramento, and Chico.
Does submitting an inquiry confirm a booking?
No. Submitting an inquiry simply starts a conversation. It does not create a reservation or confirm an event. We follow up personally to learn about your tournament and see whether we're the right fit.

When you're ready, we'd love to hear about the tournament you're planning and the day you want your players to remember.
