Some Moments Deserve Their Own Ritual.

A milestone birthday. An anniversary. A graduation, a retirement, an occasion that simply matters. The celebration belongs to the people in the room, and an attended cigar experience gives them a place to gather, slow down, and mark it together.

Most celebrations pass in a blur of toasts and photographs. Then there are the ones you want to hold onto a little longer: the birthday that closes a decade, the anniversary that has quietly earned itself, the night the family is finally in one place. Some occasions are worth marking differently.

A private celebration is not about extravagance. It is about the people who showed up, and the reason they came. An attended cigar experience is a way to honor that, a small, unhurried ritual set inside your night, chosen because the people gathered matter to you.

Where the Celebration Finds Its Center.

Every celebration has a stretch where the schedule loosens and people finally settle in. That is where the experience lives. It gives the evening a focal point, and a reason for guests to linger a while longer, past the hour a party usually begins to thin.

An ambassador brings the cigars, and the ease. Guests choose one, have it cut and lit, and fall into the kind of conversation that only happens when no one is in a hurry. We plan it with you from the first conversation, and the experience is attended throughout, so it settles into the celebration and becomes its center of gravity.

How It Works

From First Note to Final Light.

I

The conversation

We begin by learning the occasion, who is gathering, and the feeling you want the night to have. There is no script, just a conversation about your celebration.

II

The selection

We help you choose cigars suited to the occasion, your guests, and the budget you have in mind, with one thoughtfully selected cigar for every guest.

III

The celebration

We arrive and set the experience within your event. An ambassador stays through the night, helping each guest choose, cut, and light, and keeping it all easy.

IV

The moment

Your guests gather around it at their own pace. It becomes a warm part of the celebration, there for whoever wants it, and quietly remembered afterward.

No Experience Required.

Many of the people at a private celebration have never held a cigar, and that is part of what makes the moment feel open. The ambassador is there to make it easy: to help each guest choose, to cut and light by hand, and to show how to enjoy a cigar comfortably, without ceremony or pressure.

It should feel like being welcomed, not instructed. Women and men, longtime cigar lovers and complete first-timers, all find their way to it in their own time. Everyone is invited, and no one is ever obligated.

Some Come for the Cigar. Others for the Conversation.

An attended cigar experience never takes over a celebration. It becomes a natural gathering point within it, a place people drift toward as the evening softens and the night turns unhurried. Some guests come for the cigar. Others stay for the conversation that happens around it.

The host does not need to know anything about cigars, and neither do the guests. What the experience really offers is a reason to slow down together, in the middle of an evening that matters to the people in it.

Before You Reach Out

Questions Hosts Ask.

What types of private celebrations does Simply Sexy Cigars work with?
Milestone birthdays, anniversaries, graduations, engagement and retirement celebrations, and private family or estate gatherings, across Northern California. These are examples; if you're marking something that matters, we're glad to talk it through.
Do guests need cigar experience?
No. Many people at a private celebration have never held a cigar, and that is part of what makes the moment feel open. Our ambassadors help each guest choose, cut, and light, and make first-timers comfortable from the start.
How does the experience fit into a private party?
It becomes a natural gathering point within your celebration, not the whole party. Guests come and go at their own pace, some for the cigar and some for the conversation around it. No one is ever obligated to take part.
How are cigars selected?
Every celebration begins with a conversation. We help you choose a selection suited to the occasion, your guests, and the budget you have in mind, with one thoughtfully selected cigar for every guest.
Does every guest receive a cigar?
We plan one thoughtfully selected cigar for each adult guest taking part in the cigar experience. Participation is always optional, and the ambassador makes it easy for those who would like to.
Can Simply Sexy Cigars work at a private residence or estate?
Yes. The experience is designed to come to your celebration, and private homes and estate settings are a natural fit. We talk through the space and what works best during planning.
Where does Simply Sexy Cigars serve private celebrations?
We serve Northern California, from San Francisco and Silicon Valley to Napa, Sonoma, Sacramento, and Chico.
Is cigar participation 21 and older?
Yes. Simply Sexy Cigars' cigar experiences and cigar participation are intended for adults age 21 and older.
Does submitting an inquiry confirm a booking?
No. Submitting an inquiry simply starts a conversation. It does not create a reservation or confirm an event. We follow up personally to learn about your celebration and see whether we're the right fit.

When you're ready, we'd love to hear about the occasion you're marking and the people you're gathering.
