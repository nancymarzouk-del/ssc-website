A cigar experience is the unexpected touch a fundraiser is rarely offered: an attended setting, tended by an ambassador who cuts and lights by hand, that becomes the warm corner of the evening guests drift toward on their own. It is not a vendor table. It is hospitality, quietly presented, and it belongs to your cause the moment it arrives.

This is the page for the people who shape those evenings: foundation teams, event planners, tournament directors, benefit committees, and the sponsors who help make an occasion feel special. What follows is how the experience works, where it fits, and the ways it can be brought to a gala, a tournament, a benefit dinner, or a donor gathering across Northern California.

Give Them Something Worth Talking About.

Every worthy cause competes for the same evenings on the same calendars. The galas can blur together; the auctions follow a familiar rhythm; guests can often predict the shape of the night before they arrive. What they remember, what they mention to the friend they bring next year, is the thing they didn't see coming.

An attended cigar experience is exactly that: a moment guests weren't expecting, offered with genuine hospitality. It draws people together, gives the room a second gravity away from the program, and leaves the kind of impression a cause is glad to have attached to its name. It is not the point of the evening. It is the part of it guests describe afterward.

Two Ways to Bring It

The Experience Comes to the Event.

The mobile cart.

The experience comes to your guests. It is especially at home on a golf course, where players reach a designated point and find the cart waiting, an unexpected stop in the middle of the day. It can also move through a reception or roam a benefit as a warm, wandering presence.

The stationary setting.

A tended cigar setting, placed with intention: a gala terrace, a reception, an after-dinner gathering, a clubhouse, a casino floor, or an awards or donor reception. It is designed to feel integrated into the evening rather than dropped into it.

Many events choose one. Some use both: the cart through the day, the tended setting once the evening gathers.

Discovered, or Anticipated.

Some hosts prefer the experience to arrive unannounced, a quiet surprise guests come upon during the evening, and talk about on the way home. It becomes the moment no one saw on the run of show.

Others choose to feature it in advance, as one more reason the night feels considered. Either way, it gives guests one more reason to look forward to the evening, and the evening one more thing to be remembered by.

Part of the Larger Evening.

An attended cigar experience rarely stands alone. On the right evening it becomes part of something larger: a VIP reception, a donor lounge, a hosted hour guests remember as one of the warmest moments of the night.

For organizations already working with sponsors or hospitality partners, there may be an opportunity to weave the experience into that larger guest experience, a considered moment a partner is glad to be associated with. If a sponsorship or a broader hospitality idea is already part of your event, we'd be glad to explore how the experience might fit.

How It Works

From First Note to Final Light.

I

The conversation

We begin by learning your event: the cause, the venue, the guest list, and the feeling you want the room to carry home. Your committee and planner are welcome at every step.

Before You Reach Out

Questions Organizers & Planners Ask.

What is a charity cigar experience?
An attended luxury cigar experience brought to a fundraising event: a tended cigar setting, or a mobile cart, where an ambassador cuts and lights by hand. It becomes the unexpected, social corner of the evening that guests gather around and remember.
Is it only for guests who smoke cigars?
No. Most guests who gather come for the setting and the conversation as much as the cigar. The ambassador is welcoming to everyone, and the experience is built around the gathering, not the smoke.
What kinds of charity events does it suit?
Galas and benefit dinners, charity golf tournaments, casino and poker nights, auctions and vintner benefits, donor appreciation, and private foundation or estate events, across Northern California.
Can the experience come to a charity golf tournament?
Yes. Our mobile cart can meet players at a designated point on the course, an unexpected stop in the middle of the round, and it can also serve registration, the clubhouse, or the awards reception.
Is the mobile cart or the stationary setting right for us?
Either, and we help you decide. The mobile cart brings the experience to your guests; the stationary setting integrates it into a gala, reception, casino floor, or donor gathering. Some events use one; some use both.
Do you provide a cigar sponsor, or sponsored cigars?
We do not include a sponsor. That said, a sponsor could choose to underwrite or present the experience, or it could become part of a larger hospitality activation. These are possibilities we are glad to explore with you.
Can we feature the experience in advance?
Yes. Some hosts keep it a surprise guests discover during the evening; others promote it beforehand as one more reason the night feels considered. Both work well.
Do you support causes with special arrangements?
We care about the causes these evenings are built for, and every event is different. Tell us about the cause you're supporting, and we'll explore together what may be possible for your event.
Are you insured and professional?
Yes. Every experience is fully insured, discreet, and self-sufficient. The ambassador is experienced and unobtrusive, and we coordinate directly with your committee, planner, or venue.
Which areas do you serve, and how does pricing work?
We serve Northern California: Napa Valley, Sonoma County, and the greater San Francisco Bay Area, with destinations beyond by arrangement. Each event is tailored, so we prepare a considered proposal rather than a fixed package.
