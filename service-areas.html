Mobile Cigar Experiences Across Northern California.

Simply Sexy Cigars brings its attended cigar experience directly to events throughout Northern California and the San Francisco Bay Area — from the coast to the wine country to the capital region. Wherever your celebration is being held, the experience comes to you.

Simply Sexy Cigars is not a place you visit. It is an experience that arrives. We bring a tended cigar cart, a cedar-lined humidor, and an ambassador who stays with the moment, offering each guest one thoughtfully selected cigar and helping them choose, cut, and light it. The whole of it is built to travel, so the only address that matters is the one on your invitation.

That is what makes the map below simple. Across Northern California and the Bay Area, the settings change — a city rooftop, a hillside club, a vineyard terrace, a family home — but the experience is the same considered piece of hospitality, brought to wherever you are gathering. What follows is a closer look at the regions we serve and the kinds of occasions each one tends to bring.

Where We Serve

Across the Region.

Each corner of Northern California celebrates a little differently. Here is how the experience tends to find its place in each.

San Francisco & the Peninsula.

The city's celebrations lean polished and vertical — rooftop receptions, waterfront venues, and hotel ballrooms where an outdoor terrace is the prize of the evening. The cart settles naturally into that kind of setting, giving a refined corporate reception or a city wedding a warm place for guests to step out, gather, and let the night open up.

The East Bay.

From the hills above the bay to the neighborhoods below, the East Bay marks its occasions at home and at the club. It is a natural fit for private celebrations on a back terrace, corporate gatherings that want to feel less like an office, and country-club and golf-day events where the experience meets guests out in the open air.

Silicon Valley & the South Bay.

Here the calendar runs on milestones — a company anniversary, a leadership offsite, a thank-you owed to the clients who matter most. The experience reads as a considered gesture among a discerning crowd, at ease at an executive reception or client appreciation evening, and just as at home at a South Bay wedding.

Napa Valley & Sonoma County.

Wine country is already built for lingering, and the experience belongs to that unhurried rhythm. On a vineyard terrace or a private estate, it becomes the golden-hour gathering point of a wedding or a hosted weekend — a place guests drift toward as the light softens over the rows.

Sacramento & Surrounding Communities.

The capital region keeps a full and varied calendar, and the experience moves easily across it: a garden or barn wedding, a charity golf tournament, a corporate evening downtown, a fundraising gala, or a milestone birthday at home. Whatever the occasion, it arrives ready to become the part guests remember.

Chico & Greater Northern California.

Beyond the metros, the celebrations get more personal — foothill weddings, community and charitable events, and family gatherings in the smaller cities of the north. This is home ground for Simply Sexy Cigars, and destinations across the broader region are welcomed by arrangement.

Questions Hosts Ask.

Where does Simply Sexy Cigars provide its cigar experiences?
Throughout Northern California and the San Francisco Bay Area, including San Francisco and the Peninsula, the East Bay, Silicon Valley and the South Bay, Napa Valley and Sonoma County, the Sacramento area, and Chico. Destinations beyond these are welcomed by arrangement.
Does Simply Sexy Cigars come to my venue?
Yes. Simply Sexy Cigars is a mobile event experience, so the experience comes to you. We bring the cart, the humidor, and an ambassador to wherever your event is being held.
What kinds of events does Simply Sexy Cigars serve?
Weddings, golf tournaments, corporate and client appreciation events, charitable and fundraising events, and private celebrations such as milestone birthdays and anniversaries. Each is shaped around the occasion and the guests.
