# THE JOURNAL — EDITORIAL ROADMAP & GOVERNING DOCUMENT
*The single source of truth for The Simply Sexy Cigars Journal — the editorial calendar, content architecture, search strategy, and publishing law of a luxury magazine, in one place.*

**Version 1.0 — Approved and in force, 1 August 2026.** The governing editorial standard for The Simply Sexy Cigars Journal. Every future Feature commission consults it before drafting (Decision Framework, §3). It is a **living document** (§17) — every commissioned and published article updates it — but revisions are limited to meaningful editorial decisions, not continual restructuring.

### How this document works
It governs **strategy, sequence, and publishing law**. It does not touch *voice*, which is frozen elsewhere. Read **§1–§3** before commissioning anything; the rest is reference.

- **`EDITORIAL-STYLE-GUIDE.md`** (v1.0, frozen) — the voice. *Supreme authority; in any tension, the Guide wins.*
- **`EDITORIAL-REVIEW-RUBRIC.md`** (v1.0, frozen) — the pre-publication quality gate.
- **`EDITORIAL-PRODUCTION-CHECKLIST.md`** (v1.0, frozen) — imagery & technical readiness.

- **`JOURNAL-MANIFEST.md`** — the live index of what exists and its status.

> **Terminology.** **Feature** = the anchor article of a collection (the broad, defining piece it is built around). **Companion Features** = the articles that surround and support a Feature, each owning a narrower facet. All SEO planning is preserved, but it lives *inside* the editorial structure. *(Separately: the Style Guide reserves the reader-facing word **Edition** for seasonal publications; here, **Collection** is an internal planning unit only.)*

# PART I — AT A GLANCE

## 1 · EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
*The whole strategy in one page.*

**What the Journal is.** The house's literature — a luxury magazine that happens to belong to a mobile cigar company. Beautiful first, strategic second. It earns attention the way *Robb Report* or *Departures* does: by being genuinely worth reading, and by disappearing as advertising.

**Mission.** Make a reader feel the evening before they ever think about booking one. By being worth reading — never by selling — the Journal supports six goals at once: SEO, AI-search visibility, brand authority, customer trust, lead generation, and luxury positioning. It is the site's topical-authority engine, feeding readers and link authority **down** to the money pages (homepage, Experience, Contact), which it supports and never competes with.

**Editorial philosophy.** Voice is governed by the frozen Style Guide — *reveal, never explain; the brand almost disappears; one soft invitation at the very edge.* Strategy is governed by five principles (§6): **Sequence Discipline · Depth Before Breadth · Every Article Has a Job · Luxury First · Evergreen Before Trends.** In short: we build one collection at a time, to depth, and never publish incomplete work.

**The collections.** Eight, each a **Feature surrounded by Companion Features**: Weddings · Corporate Hospitality · Charity & Philanthropy · Golf & Country Clubs · Luxury Entertaining · Pairings · Cigar Education · Seasonal Experiences. Local coverage (Bay Area, Napa, Tri-Valley, Sacramento) threads through them rather than standing alone. The target library is roughly **75 articles**, built over several years at ~one publication per month.

**Publishing workflow.** Commission only through the Decision Framework (§3) → write to the Style Guide → pass the Review Rubric → pass the Production Checklist → ship atomically via "Publish to Production." Held pieces wait for their own dedicated photography; nothing incomplete goes live.

**How we measure success.** Per collection: completion % and a status ladder (*Seeded → Emerging → Authoritative → Definitive*); head-term rankings; AI-citation count; and booking inquiries attributed to the collection. Above all, the one editorial test: *would a reader forward this to a friend who will never book us?*

## 3 · DECISION FRAMEWORK
*Run before any Feature or Companion is commissioned. Six questions. **If any answer is "No," do not write the article** — reshape it until every answer is "Yes," or set it aside.*

1. **Does it strengthen an existing collection?** It must either deepen one of the eight (the default) or *intentionally* launch a new strategic collection. No isolated topics (Principle 2).
2. **Does it own a unique search intent?** Check the Search Intent Matrix (§10): no live or planned piece may share its **primary keyword + primary intent.** If it collides, narrow it or drop it.
3. **Does it support the business?** It must serve a named business objective — a booking audience, a recurring account, a local market, or an assisted-conversion path.
4. **Does it create topical authority?** It must slot cleanly into a Feature-and-Companions structure and interlink — not stand alone.
5. **Does it compete with an existing Feature?** If it rivals a Feature for the same head term, it is a Companion, not a Feature. Features own head terms; Companions own long-tails.
6. **Is it worthy of the Simply Sexy Cigars Journal?** Could it run, unchanged, in Robb Report or Departures, and would a reader forward it to someone who will never book us? If not, it is not for the Journal.

*Record the six answers, plus collection, Feature/Companion role, and intended internal links, when moving a piece Proposed → Planned (§17).*

# PART II — PURPOSE & POSITION

## 4 · MISSION

**The Journal exists to make a reader feel the evening before they ever think about booking one.** It is the house's literature — not a blog, not a content channel. The brand lives at the edges; the story is sacred (Style Guide, Principle 7). Because it is beautiful first, it does strategic work no advertisement can — six returns from one publication:

- **SEO.** The topical-authority engine. The money pages own the core commercial terms; the Journal owns the informational and long-tail space around them and passes authority and links **down** to those pages. It supports; it never competes (§12).
- **AI-search visibility (GEO).** Structured, quotable, schema-clean prose is what answer engines cite (§14).
- **Brand authority.** Publish to the standard of the luxury titles and be read as their peer. Demonstrated, never claimed.
- **Customer trust.** Restraint reads as confidence. A brand that won't sell inside its own story is trusted precisely because it isn't selling.
- **Lead generation.** One soft invitation, at a piece's edge, converts a reader already imagining their own evening. The Journal warms; the money pages close.
- **Luxury positioning.** Every choice — typography, em dashes, the absence of prices — signals a tier.

## 5 · COMPETITIVE DIFFERENTIATION

Our category is crowded with content. Almost none of it is *literature*. That is the whole position.

| They publish | Their default mode | Where we differ |
|---|---|---|
| **Wedding blogs** (The Knot, local planners) | Checklists, vendor round-ups, "27 ideas for…" | We never enumerate to rank. One story from *inside the evening*, reread for the feeling — not a listicle skimmed once. We own the *emotion*, not the to-do list. |
| **Cigar blogs** (Halfwheel, forums) | Reviews, ratings, specs, connoisseur gatekeeping | We are **never about the cigar.** No scores, no jargon, no gatekeeping — we write for the host and guest, not the collector. |
| **Event-planning blogs** (SEO farms) | Utilitarian, keyword-first, interchangeable | Our practical pieces carry the same restraint as our features. Logistics rendered with taste. |
| **Corporate-hospitality blogs** (B2B agencies) | Corporate-speak, ROI decks, "elevate your event" | The Kill List stands between us and them. We sell trust by refusing to sell. |
| **Luxury lifestyle magazines** (Robb Report, Departures) | Our *quality* peers — cinematic, editorial | We match their register but with **one focus and a service behind it.** We are the luxury magazine you can *book*. |

**Our position, in one line:** *the only publication that writes about the luxury cigar experience as literature in service of hospitality — approachable, never technical; emotional, never transactional; local enough to book, beautiful enough to forward.* No competitor holds the intersection of **literary voice × single-experience depth × Northern California deliverability.**

## 6 · EDITORIAL PRINCIPLES
*The foundation beneath every publication. Where a later section gives a rule, these give the reason.*

**1 · Sequence Discipline.** The Journal is built **one collection at a time.** Each Feature earns **two to four Companion Features** before a new collection opens. Authority comes from depth, not scatter. **Collections rank; orphan articles do not.**

**2 · Depth Before Breadth.** Strengthen existing collections before creating new ones. Every article must either deepen an existing collection or intentionally launch a new strategic one. No isolated topics that cannot grow into an authoritative series.

**3 · Every Article Has a Job.** No article exists because it is merely interesting. Each has a documented purpose — business objective, SEO objective, search intent, AI-search intent, audience, collection, internal-linking role — recorded before commissioning (the Decision Framework, §3). *If it cannot justify its role, it is not commissioned.*

**4 · Luxury First.** Editorial quality outranks publishing frequency. **We never publish incomplete work.** Writing, photography, typography, layout, and presentation all meet the house standard before publication — the reason held pieces wait, and the reason the schedule bends before the standard does.

**5 · Evergreen Before Trends.** Prioritize timeless features over trend-driven content; a piece should read as well in ten years as today. Seasonal articles **support** a collection; they never define it.

# PART III — THE COLLECTIONS

## 7 · THE COLLECTIONS & THE COLLECTION STANDARD

Eight collections organize all work. Every article belongs to **exactly one** (its home) and may bridge to others via internal links. Each collection is a **Feature surrounded by Companion Features.**

**The Collection Standard.** No collection is opened without a complete brief against these twelve fields:

> **Purpose · Mission · Audience · Business objective · SEO objective · AI-search objective · Feature · Companion Features · Estimated collection size · Current completion % · Internal-linking strategy · Status** *(plus internal-planning lines: keywords & intent · success metrics).*

**Status ladder:** *Not Started → Commissioned* (Feature written/planned, not live) *→ Seeded* (Feature live) *→ Emerging* (Feature + 2 Companions live, interlinked) *→ Authoritative* (target met, all facets covered, ≥1 AI citation) *→ Definitive* (exceeds target, owns head term, refreshed).

# PART V — THE ARCHITECTURE

## 12 · INTERNAL LINK MAP

Each collection is a **Feature surrounded by Companion Features**, with deliberate cross-collection bridges, all feeding **down** to the money pages.

```
                         MONEY PAGES  (index.html · Experience · Contact)
                                  ▲  ▲  ▲   (each Feature sends ONE soft invitation down)
        ┌──────────────┬──────────┴──┴──┴──────────┬───────────────┐
   [Weddings]     [Corporate]     [Golf & Clubs]  [Charity]     [Pairings]
    Feature        Feature         Feature         Feature       Feature
     ▲ │ │           ▲ │ │            ▲ │ │          ▲ │            ▲ │ │
  Companions     Companions       Companions     Companions    Companions
        └───── bridges ─────┴──── bridges ────┴──── bridges ──────┘
                                  │
              [Cigar Education] ── the connective tissue (links to/from every collection)
                                  │
     [Luxury Entertaining] — The Art of the Cigar Moment = GLOBAL HUB (Feature · LOCKED, inbound-only)
                                  │
                    [Seasonal Editions] cross-link season → season
```

**Rules.** (1) Feature ↔ Companion is reciprocal — the Feature links **down** via Related-Stories cards; each Companion links **up** with a descriptive in-body anchor. (2) Each Companion links across to **one** sibling. (3) Each bridge is one deliberate link. (4) The Education Feature links to/from every event collection. (5) The flagship Feature is inbound-only and **locked** — never edited to link back without approval. (6) Every Feature carries one soft invitation to the money pages; Companions may carry it once, at the edge. (7) **Held pieces are never linked from live pages** — stage a "Planned · [Collection]" non-linked card until published.

**Named bridges:** Weddings↔Golf · Weddings↔Pairings · Corporate↔Golf · Corporate↔Charity · Charity↔Golf · Seasonal↔event collections · Education↔all.

**Anti-cannibalization (the one rule):** one primary keyword per article (§10 is the check); **Features own head terms, Companions own long-tails;** the Journal never targets the money pages' transactional terms; local pieces are disambiguated by geography, not topic.

## 14 · AI SEARCH STRATEGY (GEO)

Answer engines synthesize and cite. To be cited, a page must be structured, informative, quotable, and trustworthy — which the Journal is built for.

- **Own a question.** Each article answers a real question in-prose (no FAQ). The Weddings Feature already answers five.
- **Education is the #1 GEO asset** — how-to and etiquette queries have clean, citable answers; build it for AI even before Google.
- **Definitional clarity beats keyword density** — give each concept a self-contained, extractable explanation (our pull quotes already do this).
- **Structured data + self-sufficient headings** make extraction reliable.
- **Entity authority** — consistent Organization schema + a coherent link graph + collection depth teach engines that we *are* the authority, so we are named when someone asks for a recommendation.

# PART VI — THE STANDARDS

## 15 · EDITORIAL STANDARDS (VOICE)
**Authority: `EDITORIAL-STYLE-GUIDE.md` v1.0 (frozen).** Summary only; the Guide governs.

- **Tone.** Calm, confident, restrained. Warmth without eagerness.
- **Voice.** A single unhurried host — the house itself. Second person is home key; "we" only at the edges; the brand almost disappears.
- **Reading level.** Accessible sophistication — short Anglo-Saxon words carrying large feelings. Never clinical, never dumbed-down.
- **Rhythm.** Read aloud. Vary length; short for stillness. Beware the rule-of-three tic.
- **Luxury principles.** Restraint over abundance (better usually = shorter). Place immediately. Five senses, observed. Show, never tell. Universality through emotion, never lists. Timelessness. The ending lingers.
- **CTA.** Exactly one soft invitation, at the edge only ("begin the conversation" → `index.html#booking`). Never mid-story, never with urgency or price.
- **Never:** prices/packages · CTAs inside a story · share buttons, comments, tags, filters, archives, pagination, "popular posts" · service urgency/scarcity · volume numbers, author bios · testimonials as features · superlatives (*luxurious, exquisite, unforgettable, world-class, premier, elevate, next-level*) · clichés · emoji · exclamation points · ALL-CAPS · lists that narrow the audience.

## 16 · PUBLICATION STANDARDS (PRODUCTION LAW)
*Enacts Principle 4 — never publish incomplete work. Extends `EDITORIAL-PRODUCTION-CHECKLIST.md` and `JOURNAL-PUBLISHING-WORKFLOW.md`.*

**Status definitions.**
- **Planned** — commissioned, not written. Lives in this roadmap only.
- **Draft** — written, in review; not yet Rubric-passed. Unpublished, unlinked.
- **Held** — Rubric-passed but withheld pending its own dedicated, non-reused photography (or another named gate). Carries `robots: noindex, nofollow`; not linked from any live page; staged as a "Planned · [Collection]" card.
- **Published** — live at its canonical `www` URL, in the sitemap, reachable from the Journal front door.
- **Archived** — retired, **never deleted or orphaned:** a `noindex` page or a 301 to its successor/collection (as `autumn-is-cigar-season.html` → `autumn.html`). *Locked* is a separate modifier: Publisher-approved, read-only.

**Photography.** Warm, cinematic, low-lit, human (golden→blue hour, candlelight/firelight, real occasions). Every image — real or `.ph` placeholder — carries the ten-field brief (**Subject · Mood · Time of Day · Lighting · Composition · Lens · Color Palette · Textures · Atmosphere · Editorial Inspiration**). Placeholders share the **exact** aspect ratio of their replacement. **No logos on photography; no reused imagery between collections.** WebP (~q90); `.jpg`/`.png` fallback for social.

**SEO checklist.** Unique house-voice `<title>` (SEO phrasing here, not in the H1) + meta description (~150 chars) · canonical on `www` · one primary keyword (checked against §10) · Open Graph + Twitter, images resolving to a **live** asset · descriptive kebab-case slug and image `alt` · internal links up to the Feature, across to one Companion, one soft CTA down; no links to held pieces.

**Accessibility checklist.** One `<h1>`, logical heading order, each heading self-sufficient · semantic landmarks + ARIA where needed · descriptive alt, decorative elements `aria-hidden` · keyboard-navigable, visible focus, WCAG-AA contrast · `prefers-reduced-motion` respected; content visible without JS.

**Structured data.** `@graph` = **Organization** + **Article** (`headline`, `description`, `image`, `articleSection`, `keywords`, dates, `inLanguage`, `author`/`publisher`, `isPartOf`) + **BreadcrumbList**. Local pieces add `areaServed`/`LocalBusiness`. Must validate; image URLs return 200.

**Performance.** Preserve the site's Lighthouse posture. Hero `fetchpriority="high"`; other images `loading="lazy"` + `decoding="async"`; responsive WebP; no unnecessary JS; fonts preconnected; clean at desktop and mobile.

**Publication workflow (live issues).** The atomic 13-item "Publish to Production" (`JOURNAL-PUBLISHING-WORKFLOW.md`), triggered only by the Publisher's exact phrase. Article + `journal.html` + carousel + department + Related + prev/next + Manifest + sitemap + verification + deploy ship together, or the publish is *incomplete*.

**Activation workflow (Held → Published).** Run the piece's Publication Checklist in `JOURNAL-MANIFEST.md`: replace `.ph` placeholders with final WebP; flip `robots` to `index, follow, max-image-preview:large`; update `og:image` + Article-schema image; promote the staged carousel card Upcoming→Published; add the sitemap entry; add reciprocal internal links (never to locked pages); verify links; deploy; submit the URL in Google Search Console; verify homepage reachability; run Lighthouse; confirm no broken links. Report *incomplete* if any step fails.
