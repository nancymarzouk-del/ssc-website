A Different Kind of Client Appreciation.

The most memorable corporate hospitality isn't unwrapped. It's an attended cigar experience your guests gather around, slow down for, and talk about long after the evening is over.

Northern California Client Appreciation · Receptions · Milestones · Dealerships
Begin the Conversation

People rarely remember a corporate event for its agenda. They remember how it felt: whether they were welcomed, whether they lingered, whether something about the evening surprised them. A cigar experience gives an event exactly that, a warm corner where guests gather on their own, slow down, and actually talk to one another.

This is the page for the people who shape those occasions: business owners and executives, event planners and executive assistants, dealerships, and the client-experience leaders who care how a night is remembered. What follows is what the experience adds, where it fits, and how it works, for client appreciation events, receptions, company milestones, and celebrations across Northern California.

Beyond the Expected.

Most client appreciation follows a familiar script: the branded gift, the predictable bottle, the basket that arrives and is set aside. It is thoughtful, and it is forgotten. What people carry with them is not another object. It is the feeling of having been genuinely hosted.

An attended cigar experience is a different kind of thank-you. It gives guests a reason to stay a little longer and a place to gather that feels considered rather than corporate. It is hospitality your guests take part in, not a logo they take home, and it becomes part of the story they tell about the night.

How It Works

From First Note to Final Light.

I

The conversation

We begin by learning your event: the occasion, the venue, the guest list, and the impression you want the evening to leave. Your planner or assistant is welcome at every step.

II

The selection

We help you choose a selection suited to the occasion, your guests, and the budget you have in mind, with one thoughtfully selected cigar for every guest.

III

The event

We arrive, set the experience, and tend it quietly through the hours it is open. An ambassador helps each guest choose, cut, and light.

IV

The memory

Your guests gather, linger, and leave with the moment they'll describe afterward. We pack down without a trace, and the evening stays exactly as it was.

An ambassador stays with the experience throughout, and it's designed to fit naturally into your event rather than take it over.

Welcoming to Everyone.

A cigar experience is not a club, and it is not only for the guests who already know cigars. Many of the people who gather have never held one, and that is part of the appeal. The ambassador is there to make it easy: to help each guest choose, to cut and light by hand, and to make a first cigar feel comfortable rather than intimidating.

Most guests come for the setting and the conversation as much as the cigar itself. It is an open, welcoming corner of the evening, and everyone is invited into it.

Every Milestone Deserves a Story.

Some celebrations are personal even inside a business. A customer takes delivery of the car they have wanted for years. A company opens its doors, marks an anniversary, or closes a chapter worth honoring. These are the moments a brand is remembered for, and they are made for an experience guests gather around.

The cigar experience turns a milestone into a moment: a hand-selected cigar, cut and lit, and a small circle of people who slow down to mark the occasion together. It is one example of how the experience fits a corporate celebration, offered with the same hospitality whatever the reason for the evening.

Before You Reach Out

Questions Hosts & Planners Ask.

What types of corporate events does Simply Sexy Cigars work with?
Client appreciation events, executive and leadership receptions, corporate dinners, company milestones and anniversaries, employee appreciation, dealership deliveries and celebrations, business networking events, and private company gatherings, across Northern California. These are examples; if you're planning something else, we're glad to talk it through.
Do guests need cigar experience?
Not at all. Many guests who gather have never had a cigar, and that's part of the appeal. Our ambassadors help each guest choose, cut, and light, and make first-timers feel comfortable from the start. Most people come for the setting and the conversation as much as the cigar.
How does the experience work at a client appreciation event?
The experience arrives as a tended setting within your event. An ambassador presents a cigar for each guest, helps them choose, cut, and light, and stays with the experience throughout. It becomes a natural place for guests to gather and talk, without pulling focus from the rest of the evening.
Can the experience work for dealership or milestone celebrations?
Yes. A dealership delivery, a company anniversary, a groundbreaking, or any milestone worth marking is a natural fit. The experience gives guests a moment to gather around and a story to take home. Every milestone deserves a story.
How are cigars selected?
Every event begins with a planning conversation. We work with you to choose a selection suited to the occasion, your guests, and the budget you have in mind, with one thoughtfully selected cigar for every guest.
Where does Simply Sexy Cigars serve events?
We serve Northern California, from San Francisco and Silicon Valley to Napa, Sonoma, Sacramento, and Chico.
Does submitting an inquiry confirm a booking?
No. Submitting an inquiry simply starts a conversation. It does not create a reservation or confirm an event. We follow up personally to learn about your occasion and see whether we're the right fit.

When you're ready, we'd love to hear about the occasion you're planning and the guests you want to thank.
