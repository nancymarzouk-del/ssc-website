The Experience

A gathering point guests naturally come back to.

There is something about a thoughtfully presented cigar that slows an event down in the best way. Guests linger, conversation continues, and the experience becomes part of the story of the day.

Simply Sexy Cigars is built around that moment. We provide carefully selected cigars, knowledgeable service and a polished presentation designed to complement the event around it.

The goal is simple: make the experience effortless for the host and memorable for the guests.
