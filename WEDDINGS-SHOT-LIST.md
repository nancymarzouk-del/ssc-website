# WEDDINGS ISSUE — PHOTOGRAPHY SHOT LIST
*The production creative brief for the Weddings collection of The Simply Sexy Cigars Journal. A working document for the photographer / production team — not AI prompts. Realizes the art direction in `WEDDINGS-COLLECTION-BLUEPRINT.md` §9.*

**Status: DRAFT · uncommitted.** Planning only — no production files changed, nothing committed.

---

## HOW TO USE THIS BRIEF
- **35 images across 9 stories**, numbered in the collection's reading order (§9.6 of the blueprint). Each Feature has its **own** photographic identity — do not shoot them alike.
- Every image lists: **Subject · Mood · Time of day · Light · Location · Wardrobe · Props · Composition · Lens · Orientation · Placement · Purpose**, and whether it is a **HERO** or **SUPPORTING** image. **★ = the story's signature image** (the one frame that should become synonymous with the piece).
- **Visual rhythm target ≈ 70 / 20 / 10** (daylight / golden hour / evening). As listed this brief runs **≈ 71 / 17 / 11** (tally at the end) — favor daylight coverage on the day to hold ~70%. The cigar lounge is the day's **conclusion**, not its visual language: evening frames are reserved for the Feature's close and the After-Party.

## NON-NEGOTIABLES (house standard — Roadmap §16)
- Warm, cinematic, human. Real occasions, unposed where possible — *reveal, never perform.*
- **No brand logos in frame, ever.** The brand is the feeling, not a watermark.
- **No imagery reused between collections** — this is the Weddings shoot; its frames are used nowhere else.
- Deliver **WebP (~q90)** plus RAW; heroes retouched for full-bleed. Color: warm, filmic, low-contrast blacks; never cold or clinical.
- Reference register: *Robb Report · Departures · Vogue Weddings* (+ *Kinfolk* for restraint). No stocky, over-lit "vendor" looks.

## CASTING & LOCATIONS
- **Talent:** a real-feeling couple; a small, multi-generational, diverse guest group (grandparent through twenties); one attendant (elegant, unbranded attire). Natural, lived-in elegance over model-perfect.
- **Locations (3):** (1) a **luxury hotel suite** with large windows — morning prep; (2) a **Northern California estate / vineyard** with a lawn, terrace, and valley view — the daytime & golden material; (3) the **same estate after dark** — café-lit lounge for the evening payoff. An autumn corner (turning foliage / firepit) for the seasonal frames.

## PRODUCTION GROUPING (shoot-day logistics — capture by light, not by story order)
1. **Morning block (bright window light):** Images 03–06 (Groom's Hour), 07–10 (styled lounge, daylight).
2. **Midday–afternoon block (daylight):** 11–14 (estate/vineyard), 15/17/18 (reception), 19/20/22/24 (Feature daytime), 29–31 (autumn afternoon), 33–35 (keepsakes & send-off table).
3. **Golden-hour block:** 01–02 (welcome dinner), 16 (split-frame), 23 (terrace), 27 (after-party turn), 32 (firelight).
4. **Blue-hour / evening block (the payoff):** 21, 25 (Feature lounge & cover), 26, 28 (after-party).
